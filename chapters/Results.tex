\subsection{Pauli-X gate}
Function used following \cite{sahuNoisyBraidingMajorana2026} when exchanging Majoranas 2 and 3 in the Pauli-X gate as seen in their Fig 1C illustrations 2 and 3 (Burde lage min egen figur - hvordan gjør jeg det pent?). We use $\mu_n(\tau)$ from their appendix, where we used the helper function $r(\tau)$.
\begin{align}
  r(\tau) = \left\{
  \begin{aligned}
    & 0 & \tau \leq 0 \\
    & \tau^2 (3 - 2 \tau)  & 0 < \tau < 1\\
    & 1 & \tau \geq 1
  \end{aligned} 
\right.
\end{align}
Together with their time-dependent definition\footnote{This is not exactly the definition \cite{sahuNoisyBraidingMajorana2026} used; their calling of $r(\tau)$ also allows for a spatially staggered site-by-site ramp using a parameter $\alpha$. This is however only of use when $N > 10$ as for $N=10$ we only ramp 1 site on each arm.}
\begin{align}
  \mu_n(\tau) &= \mu_\text{triv} + \left(\mu_\text{topo} - \mu_\text{triv} \right) r(\tau) \\
    \to \mu_n(\tau) &= \left\{
  \begin{aligned}
    & \mu_\text{triv}  & \tau \leq 0 \\
    & \mu_\text{triv} + \left( \mu_\text{topo} - \mu_\text{triv} \right) r(\tau)  & 0 < \tau < 1\\
    & \mu_\text{topo} & \tau \geq 1.
  \end{aligned}
\right.
    \label{eq:mu_from_sahuNoisyBraiding}
\end{align}
We here wish to check that the Pauli-x operation is realistic. Using the same parameters for $\mu_n$ as shown in \cite{sahuNoisyBraidingMajorana2026} we get Figure\ref{fig:Pauli_x_gate_1_negative_delta}, \ref{fig:Pauli_x_gate_params_1_negative_delta}. We here see that we get the expected four-fold degeneration of the ground state, and there is no energy splitting of the ground state during the time evolution. This means that the information stored in the Majoranas is protected during the operation as there is no disproportionate change in phase.(? - ref. Joakim viste noe med en energi avhengig fase for majoranaene $|0_L\rangle = e^{\phi_1(E_1)}|\gamma_1\rangle + e^{\phi_2(E_2)}|\gamma_2\rangle $) 

When using the same $\mu_n$, but using $\Delta = 1$ for the whole chain we get $8$ degeneracies of the ground state, which is unexpected. Although they also remain degenerate throughout the time evolution this is unexpected as it means there are 8 majorana zero modes for two topological chains. We believe this is due to Josephson currents effectivelly cutting the T-junction into separate chains at the central site(? - Noe å ta tak i når jeg har lest på Josephson currents)
